chair game

You've just entered the train.
It's packed in here.
Speed is needed to get you the thing you want the most —
a place near the window.

You scan anxiously left to right
and suddenly you see it.
There it is, a couple of steps ahead of you — an empty seat.
But of course, you're not the only one trying to sit.

How to play:

Click the chairs or press keys 1–7 to play notes.
Press ENTER to record/playback your sequence.
Double-click the canvas to stop rotation.

This is musical chairs for passengers.
Compose your own subway tune.
